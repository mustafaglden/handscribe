"Compose SOAP note" is greyed out

On-device drafting needs Apple Intelligence: iPhone 15 Pro or later with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri. On other iPhones you can still dictate and write notes manually.

Dictation doesn't pick up my words

Allow the microphone in Settings → Handscribe. The first dictation may download the English speech model; keep the phone close and unmuted. You can always edit the transcript before composing.

Where are my notes stored?

Only on your iPhone. Use Settings → Export all data for a ZIP with JSON and PDFs. Include Handscribe in your iCloud or computer backups if you want a copy elsewhere.

Purchases and restore

Pro is managed by Apple. Settings → Restore Purchases re-checks your subscription; Manage subscription opens the App Store.
