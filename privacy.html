Handscribe Privacy Policy

Effective date: 24 September 2026

Handscribe stores everything you enter — clients, intake details, session notes, body-map marks, signatures and exported PDFs — only on your iPhone. None of it is uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Dictation and AI

When you dictate a note, the audio is transcribed on your iPhone by Apple's on-device speech recognition and immediately discarded; Handscribe never records or stores audio. Notes you type instead of dictating are handled the same way. The SOAP draft is composed on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model. Your dictation, typed notes and finished notes are not sent to a server. On iPhones without Apple Intelligence, "Split into SOAP" sorts your notes by keywords, also on the device.

Analytics

To improve the app we collect anonymous usage analytics and crash reports through Google Firebase (for example, which screens are used, how long composing took, and whether the app crashed). This data is not linked to your identity, is not used for advertising, and never contains client names, note text, or transcripts. Handscribe shows no ads and does not require an account.

Purchases

Subscriptions and the lifetime unlock are processed by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details.

Your data, your exit

You can export everything (JSON and PDFs) or delete everything from Settings at any time. An encrypted backup (Settings → Backup & Restore) is a file locked with a password you choose; it is saved wherever you decide to share it, and we never receive the file or the password. If you turn on automatic iCloud backup, that file is written once a day to your own iCloud Drive (folder Handscribe) and the password is kept in your iCloud Keychain; both stay in your Apple account and never reach us. If you delete the app, its data is deleted with it, subject to the device backups you control.

Your clients' data

You are responsible for handling your clients' information in line with the laws that apply to your practice. Handscribe is a record-keeping tool; it does not transmit client information anywhere.
