Handscribe

Dictate or type the session. Get the SOAP note.

Made for massage therapists, physiotherapists and hands-on practitioners. Speak for a minute after the session or type a few short notes; Handscribe turns them into a clean, editable SOAP note — on your iPhone, and your notes are never uploaded.

Privacy PolicySupport

How it works

  • Dictate or type. Say what they reported, what you found, what you did, what's next — or type short notes in any order when talking isn't an option.
  • Built for your practice. Massage therapy, physiotherapy and general bodywork templates, pain before and after (0–10), and phrases you insert with one tap.
  • On your phone. Transcription and drafting run on-device with Apple Intelligence. Audio is never recorded or stored.
  • A real chart. Intake with consent signature, tap-to-mark body map, techniques and pressure, timeline per client.
  • PDFs when you need them. Single-session SOAP PDF free; whole-chart PDF with Pro. Export everything any time.
  • Every iPhone. With Apple Intelligence the AI drafts the note; on any other iPhone, Split into SOAP sorts it by the section names you say.
  • Progress you can see. Pain before and after with a trend across sessions; edited notes keep their earlier versions.
  • Your records stay yours. Automatic daily backup to your own iCloud Drive, encrypted with your password, and a backup file to move to a new iPhone.
  • In your language. English, Turkish, German, Spanish, French, Portuguese (Brazil), Italian and Japanese.

Requirements

iOS 26 or later. On-device drafting requires an iPhone with Apple Intelligence (iPhone 15 Pro or later); manual notes work on every iPhone running iOS 26, and dictation wherever on-device speech recognition supports your language.
